\subsection{Detector Matrix}
\label{sec:setup-detectors}

The matrix spans four families, all consuming identical graphs. \emph{Rules} encode the auditor's prior directly: a call targeting a sensitive sink without an upstream authorizing read or confirmation flags the graph, and calls outside the task's declared grant likewise. \emph{Shallow models over graph features} (IsolationForest, logistic regression from the PyOD toolbox~\cite{zhao2019pyod}) consume hand-crafted whole-graph statistics: tool histograms, sink-touching counts, flow-path depths, and temporal regularity. \emph{Sequence models} ignore the graph: an $n$-gram model over tool-call tokens, a dual-head trajectory transformer in the spirit of DriftNet~\cite{pinjari2026driftnet}, and an LLM judge that reads the textual trajectory and scores it, representing the per-trace version of current practice~\cite{yuan2024rjudge,li2026unsafer}. \emph{Graph neural detectors} include node-level GAD repurposed to whole-graph scores by pooling node anomalies (DOMINANT and CoLA from PyGOD~\cite{liu2022pygod}), zero-shot graph-level methods AnomalyGFM and OWLEYE~\cite{qiao2025anomalygfm,zheng2026owleye}, the transferable graph-level detector GLASS~\cite{wang2026glass}, a supervised heterogeneous GNN with graph pooling as an upper bound, and our reference detector [TBD---detector name and one-line architecture from user's draft], trained with the family labels subject to LOAO restriction.
